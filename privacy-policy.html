Effective Date: November 13, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Data Egress Architecture

SpaceBatch ("the App") is built on the Atlassian Forge platform as a 100% client-side Custom UI application eligible for the official Runs on Atlassian program.

  • No External Servers: The App does not operate, connect to, or transmit data to any external backend servers, third-party APIs, or external domains (permissions.external is completely empty).
  • No Data Storage: The App does not store, log, or persist any Confluence page content, titles, labels, user identifiers, or personal data in any external or hosted database.
  • Browser-Only Processing: All page queries, CQL searches, dry-run diff calculations, batch label/page updates, and CSV/JSON exports execute strictly inside the authenticated administrator's web browser memory and communicate exclusively with your own Atlassian Cloud instance via @forge/bridge.

2. Personal Data & GDPR / CCPA Compliance

Because the App neither transmits nor stores customer data outside your Atlassian Cloud tenant, the vendor does not collect, process, or retain any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) or user-generated content. All data residency, encryption in transit and at rest, and access controls remain 100% governed by your organization's existing Atlassian Cloud agreement.

3. Security & Vulnerability Reporting

For privacy, security, or compliance inquiries, please contact the vendor via the support channels listed on our Support & SLA page.
